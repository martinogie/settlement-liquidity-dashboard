    How it works

    PassRuleBatchesShare
    • Liquidity: merchants are paid on schedule even when a network settles late, so a delay is funded from our own cash. Each evening collections are swept to the payout account, which must hold 105% of tomorrow's payouts; shortfalls are topped up from operating, then from the unrestricted reserve.
    • Coverage is available cash (total less the restricted scheme reserve) divided by average daily payouts over the last 20 business days. The reserve requirement rose from $25M to $40M on 1 July, which is why coverage sits below the 3-day target for all of Q3.
    • Concentration flags any bank holding more than 45% of cash.
    • SQL: the KPIs are reproduced as SQLite queries (sql/kpis.sql), and tests check that SQL and Python agree.

    All data is synthetic: 6 months of settlement batches from Visa, Mastercard, Amex, ACH and RTP with realistic reference loss, late, short, split and netted payments, plus three injected incidents. Built with AI-assisted coding (Claude); the design, controls and review are my own.
